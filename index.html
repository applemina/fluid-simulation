Mass Flow Rate — Continuity Equation
The A₁/A₂ diameter ratio (β) is what links v₁ and v₂

Continuity (conservation of mass): A₁v₁ρ₁ = A₂v₂ρ₂. With an incompressible fluid (ρ₁ = ρ₂), this reduces to a pure area ratio: v₁ = (A₂/A₁)v₂ = β·v₂. A narrow valve relative to the tank (small β) means v₁ ≈ 0 is a good approximation; as the valve approaches the tank's own size, v₁ stops being negligible.
